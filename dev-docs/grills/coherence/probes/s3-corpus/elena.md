# Character sheet — Elena Voss

- Twenty years old (born Year 703).
- Daughter of Corin Voss, a glassblower on Saltmarket Row; her mother died
  when Elena was small.
- Grey eyes; dark hair kept in a sailor's braid. Left-handed.
- Carries her late mother's brass compass everywhere; its lid is worn
  smooth by her thumb.
- Afraid of deep water since childhood; never learned to swim.
- Temperament: slow to anger, a patient bargainer; goes quiet when hurt.
  Dry humour, precise speech.
- An unlicensed tidebinder — modest talent, self-taught, kept secret.
