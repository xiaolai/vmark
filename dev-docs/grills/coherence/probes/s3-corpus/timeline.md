# Corvel timeline

- Year 701 — the Drowning of Corvel Bay: the old harbour quarter is lost
  to the sea in a single night.
- Year 703 — Elena Voss born.
- Year 712 — the Guild Charter is signed; the Lantern Guild is granted
  enforcement powers in Corvel.
- Year 718 — the Long Fog descends in autumn.
- Year 719 — the Long Fog lifts in spring. That summer, the Ashfleet
  burns at anchor.
- Year 721 — Marcus Hale takes the Gullpoint lighthouse post.
- Year 723 — the present.
