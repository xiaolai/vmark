# Style guide

- Past tense, third-person limited. One point-of-view character per
  scene.
- Sensory-forward prose: lead with what the point-of-view character
  smells, hears, or touches before what they think.
- Prefer sentences under twenty-five words; vary the rhythm.
- No said-bookisms: use "said" or nothing.
- British spelling throughout (grey, harbour, colour, offence).
- No chapter epigraphs, no scene titles inside the prose.
