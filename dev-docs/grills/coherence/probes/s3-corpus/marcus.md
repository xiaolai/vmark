# Character sheet — Marcus Hale

- Late fifties. A retired soldier of the Ashfleet campaign.
- Keeper of the Gullpoint lighthouse since Year 721.
- An old wound left him with a limp; he favours his right leg and takes
  stairs one at a time.
- Keeps a tame raven named Pitch and talks to it like an old comrade.
- Never touches alcohol; drinks bitter kelp tea by the pot.
- Distrusts the Lantern Guild and is loud about it.
- Not a tidebinder; no talent, no wish for it.
