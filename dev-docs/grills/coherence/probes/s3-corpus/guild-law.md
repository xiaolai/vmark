# Lantern Guild charter law, as enforced in Corvel

- Only Guild-licensed binders may perform tidebinding within the city
  walls.
- Unlicensed working is punished by branding on the inside of the left
  wrist — the wave-mark. A second offence means exile.
- Guild inspectors wear grey sashes and carry iron rods under a standing
  writ of search.
- Guild apprentices are under curfew from the ninth bell.
- A licence costs forty crowns a year. Charter families hold a hereditary
  exemption.
